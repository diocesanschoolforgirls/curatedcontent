# Medicine & Science Research Hub

A Year 9 Science media literacy activity. Students research five medical topics through a small "internet" of realistic source pages that range from peer-reviewed journals to wellness blogs and sponsored content, then judge which sources they can trust.

**Live:** https://interactives.diocesan.school.nz/curatedcontent/

Static HTML, no build step, no framework. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish the folder to GitHub Pages.

## What's here

- `index.html`: the library, where students start.
- `topics/`: one hub page per topic: vaccines, antibiotics and resistance, Semmelweis and handwashing, Joseph Lister and sterilisation, how clinical trials work.
- `sources/`: the 25 source pages, five per topic, of deliberately varied quality.
- `classroom/`: printable materials for the Source Audit jigsaw lesson (40 minutes): student cards, the group ranking sheet and the teacher guide.

## Running the lesson

See `classroom/teacher-guide.html` for timings, what to say at each stage and the debrief. Do not tell students in advance that the sources vary in quality; that is the point of the activity.
